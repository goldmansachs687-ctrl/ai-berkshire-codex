## English

[Read the English PDF](alphabet-research-en.pdf) · [Read the Simplified Chinese PDF](alphabet-research-zh-CN.pdf)

The full report covers the company and its development, how Search, YouTube and Cloud earn money, ten fiscal years of financials, moat and management, cash-flow valuation, and public sell-side coverage. The appendix links twenty original SEC annual filings.

**Question. How much of Alphabet's AI and Cloud growth can become cash for common shareholders?** A customer's multi-year minimum spend makes part of the demand visible. The research also tracks Alphabet's delivery costs, inventory and receivables, equipment replacement and its exposure as a financier or investor. [Alphabet Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm) · [Anthropic S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)

| Bull/bear challenge | Controlled test in the frozen DCF | Conditional USD/share |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Can existing capacity deliver the same growth with less net capital? | At the same sales and profit path, mid-period sales per $1 of net capital rise from 0.9 to 1.2. | $134.40 → $138.63 |
| How much of Cloud growth will persist after concentrated hardware deliveries? | Initial Cloud growth falls from 40% to 30%. The growth-rate difference fades to zero by period 13. | $134.40 → $119.50 |

In the capital-efficiency test, period 8 incremental sales are $67.657 billion. Dividing by 0.9 gives $75.174 billion of net reinvestment. Dividing by 1.2 gives $56.381 billion. Using unrounded inputs, the difference adds $18.794 billion to that period's modeled cash flow. The per-share impact incorporates all affected periods and discounts them to the valuation date.

In the Cloud stress test, the model's first-period cash flow improves by $4.215 billion because working-capital needs fall by more than after-tax operating profit. The smaller revenue base reduces full-horizon value. The team kept both base inputs pending better evidence on delivery and collections, the mix of recurring services and hardware, and capital needed after assets enter service.

**Dates and review.** Research dated 13 November 2026, Hong Kong time. Financials through Q3 2026. The sensitivity tests reuse the 9 November frozen model and compare against the 6 November U.S. close. The local run used Berkshire Team 2.2.16 and AIVA 1.2.2. Four researchers and two debaters contributed. A separate reviewer recomputed six sensitivity cases. The public PDFs present the integrated report. The increment's original limited delivery reflected missing public data on AI Search cash per commercial task and the hardware share of incremental Cloud revenue. The values shown are model scenarios using historical inputs.

## 简体中文

[阅读中文 PDF](alphabet-research-zh-CN.pdf) · [阅读英文 PDF](alphabet-research-en.pdf)

完整报告涵盖公司与发展史、搜索与 YouTube 及 Cloud 的商业模式、十年财务、护城河与管理层、现金流估值和公开投行资料。附录提供二十份 SEC 原始年报入口。

**研究问题。谷歌的 AI 和 Cloud 增长，有多少能变成普通股股东的现金？** 客户的多年最低支出让一部分需求更容易验证。研究同时追踪谷歌的交付成本、库存与应收、设备更新，以及提供融资或投资客户承担的风险。[Alphabet 2026 年第三季度季报](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm) · [Anthropic 修订申报](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)

| 多空质询 | 冻结 DCF 的单变量检验 | 条件值（美元/股） |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 已建产能能否用更少净资本支持同样增长？ | 收入和利润路径保持不变，中期每投入 1 美元净资本带来的新增年收入由 0.9 升至 1.2。 | $134.40 → $138.63 |
| 集中交付硬件后，Cloud 还有多少增长可以延续？ | Cloud 首期增速由 40% 降至 30%，增速差到第 13 期收敛为零。 | $134.40 → $119.50 |

资本效率情景下，第 8 期新增收入为 676.57 亿美元。除以 0.9，净再投资为 751.74 亿美元。除以 1.2，净再投资为 563.81 亿美元。用未舍入输入计算，差额使当期模型现金流增加 187.94 亿美元。每股变化包含所有受影响期间的现金流并折现到估值日。

Cloud 承压情景下，首期营运资金占用减少的金额超过税后经营利润的下降，模型首期现金流反而改善 42.15 亿美元。后续收入基数变小，全周期价值下降。团队保留两项基准，等待交付与回款、持续服务和硬件收入占比、以及资产投用后所需资本的进一步证据。

**时点与复核。** 研究日为 2026 年 11 月 13 日香港时间，财务截至 2026 年第三季度。敏感性测试沿用 11 月 9 日冻结模型，价格对照为 11 月 6 日美东正常收盘。本地运行使用伯克希尔团队 2.2.16 与 AIVA 1.2.2，四位研究员、两位辩手参与，另由独立复核员复算六项敏感性情景。两份 PDF 对应综合稿。原增量研究按有限范围交付，公开披露仍缺 AI 搜索每次商业任务的现金贡献和 Cloud 增量收入中的硬件占比。所列数字是基于历史输入的模型情景。
