[Back to project / 返回项目](../../README.md) · [American Water worked case / 美国水务案例](american-water-blind-test.md)

The experiments ran on November 11–12, 2026 (Hong Kong time). Independent graders reviewed anonymized answers against rubrics set before grading. WM was a before-and-after exercise. The other four rounds compared agents who received the same case evidence, with a principle card supplied to one group. The card evolved between rounds, and one card-group respondent in Carnival had also taken part in FedEx. Monetary amounts are in USD millions. Reported enterprise values and project decisions use teaching scenarios.

## When the card appeared useful / 哪些情况下有可见作用

**Unresolved evidence.** American Water showed the clearest difference. Both groups noticed the gaps. The card group was more consistent in showing exactly where the payable roll-forward broke and valuing a consequential alternative. The 1,107.92 EV spread comes from how the simulated payment is classified. The first-year discrepancy by itself changes EV by 1.85.

**证据仍有缺口。** 美国水务的差异最清楚。两组都发现了问题，有卡组更一致地写清应付余额在哪里对不上，并量化了会明显改变估值的另一种归属。1,107.92 的估值差来自模拟付款的归属。首年应付差额单独只影响 1.85。

**Making the cash basis explicit.** In Union Pacific, the difference was one statement about dividends. The cash calculations and project decision agreed.

**把现金口径说清楚。** 联合太平洋铁路的分差来自是否明确写出分红前口径，现金计算和项目选择一致。

## Where we saw no difference / 哪些情况下没看出差别

**Direct evidence linking payments.** When the two tables showed the same label and amount (FedEx), or a complete mapping and reconcilable balances (Carnival), the groups reached the same valuations. Across FedEx, Carnival and American Water, observed double-counting was 0/6 with the card and 0/6 without it.

**材料能直接对上付款。** FedEx 同名同额，嘉年华虽异名跨期但给齐了对应和可勾稽的余额。两轮估值均无组间差异。合计 FedEx、嘉年华和美国水务三轮，有卡组实际双扣 0/6，无卡组也为 0/6。

**Scope.** The trials used one or two answers per group and case-specific rubrics. Scores are comparable within each round. The observed differences describe these submissions. More trials would be needed to estimate how much the card changes error rates or valuation decisions.

**适用范围。** 每轮每组只有一到两份答卷，评分表按案例制定。分数用于同轮比较。上述差异描述的是这几次作答，要估计原则卡能在多大程度上改变错误率或估值决策，还需要更多试验。
